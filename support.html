Support

Paid And Didn't
Get It? Tell Us.

Machines are machines. If one takes your money and gives you nothing — or drops the wrong thing, or charges you twice — fill in the form below and we'll put it right. No receipt needed, no argument.

You tell us what happened

One short form. The last four digits of your card are the only detail we have to have.

We check the machine

Every vend is logged electronically, so we can see the transaction and whether the product actually dropped. You hear back within one business day.

We refund your card

Money goes back to the card you tapped. Your bank usually posts it within a few business days.

Never send your full card number — to us or to anyone. The last four digits are all we need to find the transaction. Your details are used only to resolve this issue, never shared and never sold.

Before you ask

The usual questions.

How long until I get my money back?

We reply within one business day. Once the refund is issued it goes back to the card you tapped, and banks usually post it within a few business days — that last part is their timing, not ours.

I don't remember the last 4 digits of my card.

They appear beside the charge in your banking app or card statement. If you genuinely can't find them, send the form anyway with the venue and roughly when it happened, and we'll work from the transaction log.

The machine gave me the wrong item.

Tell us what you paid for and what you got. We'll refund the difference, refund the purchase, or get you the right product — whichever you'd prefer.

My item is stuck in the machine.

Don't shake or tip the unit — it's wall-mounted and heavier than it looks. Report it here and we'll refund you. Venue staff can't open the machine, so there's no point asking them.

I was charged twice.

Send the form and note it in the message. Duplicate charges are visible in the transaction log and we refund the extra charge in full.

Can I just ask the bar staff?

They can't help with this one. The venue hosts the machine but Midnight Vault runs it — the payments, the stock and the refunds are all ours. This form reaches us directly.

I'm the venue, and the machine has a problem.

Use this form too, or email us directly at midnightvault.to@gmail.com. Faults, damage and anything blocking the floor get handled first.
