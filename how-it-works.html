The partnership

You Give Us The Wall Space
We Handle The Rest.

Midnight Vault is a fully managed operation. There is no equipment to buy, no product to order, no staff task to add and no invoice to pay. The machine arrives finished and stays that way.

Midnight Vault handles
  • Machine installation and mounting
  • Product sourcing and supplier relationships
  • Inventory ownership and cost
  • Restocking visits, scheduled around your hours
  • Payment processing and transaction fees
  • Sales monitoring and reporting
  • Product optimization for your crowd
  • Machine management and software
  • Maintenance, servicing and repairs
Your venue provides
  • A wall or compact corner
  • A standard power outlet
  • Access for restocking and service

That's the entire list. Your staff never stock it, never sell from it, never handle cash for it and never troubleshoot it.

The revenue share

Your venue earns a share of every sale.

The percentage and the terms of the agreement are set with you directly, during the walkthrough and the conversation that follows. They depend on your venue — capacity, traffic, placement and hours.

Nothing is installed until the agreement is confirmed in writing and you are satisfied with it. There is no cost to your venue at any stage.

From walkthrough to live

Four steps.
Then it just runs.

01

The walkthrough

We visit your venue, look at your traffic flow and find the right placement. Twenty minutes, no obligation.

02

The lineup

We build a product mix around your clientele. An event space, a bar and a nightclub each need a different vault, and we'd rather stock yours correctly than stock it identically.

03

Installation

We mount, power and connect the unit outside your service hours, then stock it and test every transaction path. You open that night with it live.

04

Ongoing operation

We restock on a schedule set by your actual sales, service the machine when it needs it, and refine the lineup as we learn your room. You do nothing.

Before you ask

The questions venues actually ask.

What does this cost my venue?

Nothing. There is no purchase, no lease, no installation fee and no monthly charge. Midnight Vault carries the machine, the inventory and the operating cost. Your venue earns a share of sales.

How much space does it need?

Less than a poster frame in the wall-mounted configuration, and roughly the footprint of a bar stool on a compact floor stand. We choose the configuration during the walkthrough based on what your room can spare — the goal is to use space you aren't selling.

Will my staff have to do anything?

No. They don't stock it, sell from it, handle money for it or fix it. The only thing we ask is access for scheduled restocking and service, arranged around your hours rather than during them.

What happens if it breaks or a product jams?

You contact us and we handle it. Maintenance and repairs are entirely ours. Because the machine is cashless and connected, we can see a problem without waiting for someone to report it.

Is there cash in the machine?

Never. Midnight Vault is fully cashless — tap, card or phone only. There is no float, no cash box and nothing inside worth breaking it open for.

What kinds of venues is this for?

Nightclubs, bars, lounges, hotels, event spaces and other late-night hospitality venues across the Greater Toronto Area. If your guests are out for hours and can't easily leave to buy something, the vault earns.

How long does installation take?

A single visit outside your service hours. We mount, power, connect, stock and test in one session, and the unit is live for that night's service.
