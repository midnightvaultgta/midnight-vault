The hardware

Two Sizes.
One Standard.

Both machines mount to a wall and run entirely on card and contactless. Choose by the room, not by the budget — the smaller unit is not a lesser one.

Model 01

The Vault

The full unit. A taller screen, a deeper cabinet and an illuminated exit that reads across a dark room. Specified where the machine should be seen, not just found.

The Vault specifications
Width 23.6in 59.9 cm
Height 40.5in 102.9 cm
Depth 10.2in 25.9 cm
Product exit 4.33in 11.0 cm
Weight 121lb 54.9 kg

Occupies roughly 6.6 sq ft of wall — about the span of a medium mirror.

Model 02

The Vault Mini

The tighter option. Sized for whatever wall the room can spare, without asking you to give up space you would rather be selling.

The Vault Mini specifications
Width 21.6in 54.9 cm
Height 33.3in 84.6 cm
Depth 8.7in 22.1 cm
Product exit 3.93in 10.0 cm
Weight 66lb 29.9 kg

Occupies roughly 5 sq ft of wall — 7 inches shorter than The Vault.

Model 03

The Vault, Freestanding

The same unit on a mobile base. For rooms where drilling into the wall is not an option — heritage buildings, glass, leased walls — or where the vault needs to move between the lounge and the event floor.

Freestanding base specifications
Base width 25.19in 64.0 cm
Base depth 22.04in 56.0 cm
Base height 27.36in 69.5 cm
Mobility Locking casters
Mounting No wall fixing

Takes 3.9 sq ft of floor and rolls on locking casters. Overall height depends on which unit is mounted — with The Vault, it stands roughly 67.9 in / 172.4 cm to the top.
